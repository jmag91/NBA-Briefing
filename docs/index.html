Points per possession come from box scores (possessions = FGA + 0.44 x FTA - offensive rebounds + turnovers) and count regular-season games only. The shot-clock rows are an estimate from play-by-play game-clock times, not official tracking data. Season outlook, impact and projected minutes come from your forecast file; impact is a player's contribution to team strength in that model. Form uses completed regular-season games only. Injuries and officials come from ESPN and may lag. Crowd % is the Polymarket price, a rough signal when trading is thin.
